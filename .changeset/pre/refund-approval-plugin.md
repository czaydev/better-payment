Add the `refundApproval` plugin: requires manager approval before processing refunds and cancellations above a specified threshold, with in-memory or custom store, approval/rejection methods, self-approval prevention, Slack/webhook notify callback, and handler endpoints (`/refunds/pending`, `/refunds/approve`, `/refunds/reject`).
