# Docs sidebar logo sources

Small square logos next to the provider, bank and integration pages in the docs sidebar.
They are trademarks of their owners and only identify the service a page documents; no endorsement is implied.

Framework tiles (`*.svg` except `elysia.svg`) are the [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) glyphs
(CC0-1.0) on a tile in the brand colour.
